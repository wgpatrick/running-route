# Build instructions: printer

154 parts, 84 screws, about 29 minutes (rough allowance). Tools: 2.5 mm hex key, 4 mm hex key.

## Main assembly

1. Start with **frame_bot_left** on the bench.
2. Fit **frame_bot_back** onto frame_bot_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
3. Fit **upright_right** onto frame_bot_back: bring it in from above (-Z).
4. Fit **upright_left** onto frame_bot_back: bring it in from above (-Z).
5. Fit **z_motor_mount** onto upright_left, upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
6. Fit **z_motor** onto z_motor_mount: bring it in from the side (-Y). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
7. Fit **z_rail_left** onto upright_left: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
8. Fit **z_rail_right** onto upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
9. Fit **z_coupler** onto z_motor: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
10. Fit **leadscrew** onto z_coupler, z_motor: bring it in from above (-Z).
11. Fit **z_nut** onto leadscrew: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
12. Drive 2 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: z_rail_right_screw_0, z_rail_left_screw_0.
13. Fit **carrier** onto z_nut: bring it in from above (-Z).
14. Drive 2 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: z_rail_right_screw_1, z_rail_left_screw_1.
15. Fit **bed** onto carrier: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it would tip or slide off (no surface underneath it)).
16. Fit **frame_top_back** onto upright_left, upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it would tip or slide off (no surface underneath it)).
17. Fit **frame_top_right** onto frame_top_back: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
18. Fit **frame_top_left** onto frame_top_back: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
19. Fit **frame_bot_right** onto frame_bot_back: bring it in from the side (-X).
20. Fit **y_rail_left** onto frame_top_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
21. Fit **y_rail_right** onto frame_top_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
22. Drive 1 screw from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: y_rail_left_screw_3.
23. Fit **y_carriage_left** onto y_rail_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
24. Fit **y_plate_left** onto y_carriage_left: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it would tip or slide off (centre of mass is 4 mm outside what it rests on)).
25. Drive 1 screw from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: y_rail_right_screw_3.
26. Fit **y_carriage_right** onto y_rail_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
27. Fit **y_plate_right** onto y_carriage_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
28. Drive 8 screws from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: y_plate_right_screw_0, y_plate_right_screw_3, y_plate_right_screw_2, y_plate_left_screw_0, y_plate_right_screw_1, y_plate_left_screw_1, y_plate_left_screw_2, y_plate_left_screw_3.
29. Fit **x_beam** onto y_plate_left, y_plate_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
30. Fit **y_motor_mount** onto frame_top_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
31. Fit **y_motor** onto y_motor_mount: bring it in from the side (+X).
32. Fit **frame_top_front** onto frame_top_left, frame_top_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
33. Fit **frame_bot_front** onto frame_bot_left, frame_bot_right: bring it in from the side (+Y).
34. Fit **x_motor** onto y_plate_left: bring it in from below (+Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
35. Fit **post_rf** onto frame_bot_front, frame_bot_right, frame_top_front: bring it in from the side (-X).
36. Fit **post_lb** onto frame_bot_back, frame_bot_left, frame_top_back: bring it in from the side (+X).
37. Fit **post_rb** onto frame_bot_back, frame_bot_right, frame_top_back: bring it in from the side (-X).
38. Fit **post_lf** onto frame_bot_front, frame_bot_left, frame_top_front: bring it in from the side (+X).
39. Fit **x_rail** onto x_beam: bring it in from above (-Z).
40. Drive 1 screw from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: x_rail_screw_2.
41. Fit **x_carriage** onto x_rail: bring it in from above (-Z).
42. Fit **tool_plate** onto x_carriage: bring it in from above (-Z).
43. Turn the assembly on its back (front face up).
44. Fit **hotend** onto tool_plate: move it -X then +Z by 150 mm.
45. Fit **z_carriage_right** onto carrier, z_rail_right: bring it in from the side (-Z).
46. Fit **z_carriage_left** onto carrier, z_rail_left: bring it in from the side (-Z).
47. Fit **y_idler_mount** onto frame_top_right: bring it in from the side (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
48. Fit **bracket_13** onto frame_top_back, frame_top_right: bring it in from the side (-Z).
49. Fit **bracket_15** onto frame_bot_back, frame_bot_right: bring it in from the side (+Z).
50. Fit **bracket_12** onto frame_top_right, post_rb: bring it in from the side (-X).
51. Fit **bracket_5** onto frame_top_back, frame_top_left: bring it in from the side (-Z).
52. Fit **bracket_4** onto frame_top_left, post_lb: bring it in from the side (+X).
53. Turn the assembly on its front (back face up).
54. Fit **bracket_10** onto frame_bot_right, post_rf: bring it in from the side (-X).
55. Fit **bracket_14** onto frame_bot_right, post_rb: bring it in from the side (-X). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
56. Fit **bracket_0** onto frame_top_left, post_lf: bring it in from the side (-X).
57. Fit **bracket_9** onto frame_top_front, frame_top_right: bring it in from the side (-Z).
58. Fit **bracket_2** onto frame_bot_left, post_lf: bring it in from the side (+X).
59. Fit **bracket_6** onto frame_bot_left, post_lb: bring it in from the side (+X). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
60. Fit **bracket_7** onto frame_bot_back, frame_bot_left: bring it in from the side (+Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
61. Fit **bracket_8** onto frame_top_right, post_rf: bring it in from the side (-X).
62. Fit **bracket_11** onto frame_bot_front, frame_bot_right: bring it in from the side (+Z).
63. Fit **bracket_1** onto frame_top_front, frame_top_left: bring it in from the side (-Z).
64. Fit **bracket_3** onto frame_bot_front, frame_bot_left: bring it in from the side (+Z).
65. Fit **brkt_up_top_in_left** onto frame_top_back, upright_left: bring it in from above (-Y). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
66. Fit **brkt_up_top_out_right** onto frame_top_back, upright_right: bring it in from above (-Y). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
67. Turn the assembly upright (as designed).
68. Fit **brkt_up_bot_out_left** onto frame_bot_back, upright_left: bring it in from the side (-Y).
69. Fit **brkt_up_bot_in_left** onto frame_bot_back, upright_left: bring it in from the side (-Y).
70. Fit **brkt_up_top_in_right** onto frame_top_back, upright_right: bring it in from the side (-Y). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
71. Fit **brkt_up_bot_in_right** onto frame_bot_back, upright_right: bring it in from the side (-Y).
72. Fit **brkt_up_bot_out_right** onto frame_bot_back, upright_right: bring it in from the side (-Y).
73. Fit **brkt_up_top_out_left** onto frame_top_back, upright_left: bring it in from the side (-Y). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
74. Fit **x_belt_clamp** onto tool_plate: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
75. Fit **x_pulley** onto x_motor: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
76. Fit **y_pulley** onto y_motor: bring it in from the side (-X).
77. Fit **y_belt_clamp** onto y_plate_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
78. Fit **x_idler** onto y_plate_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
79. Turn the assembly on its left side (right side up).
80. Fit **y_idler** onto y_idler_mount: bring it in from the side (-Z).
81. Drive 1 screw from above with the 4 mm hex key: y_idler_bolt.
82. Drive 3 screws from the side with the 4 mm hex key: x_idler_bolt, beam_bolt_left, beam_bolt_right.
83. Drive 5 screws from above with the 4 mm hex key: y_motor_mount_bolt_2, y_idler_mount_bolt_0, y_motor_mount_bolt_0, y_motor_mount_bolt_1, y_idler_mount_bolt_1.
84. Drive 2 screws from the side with the 4 mm hex key: z_mount_bolt_right, z_mount_bolt_left.
85. Drive 28 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: nut_screw_0, nut_screw_3, nut_screw_2, nut_screw_1, x_belt_clamp_screw_2, x_belt_clamp_screw_1, x_belt_clamp_screw_0, x_motor_screw_0 ....
86. Drive 4 screws from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: y_motor_screw_2, y_motor_screw_3, y_motor_screw_0, y_motor_screw_1.
87. Drive 26 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: z_motor_screw_1, z_motor_screw_3, z_motor_screw_2, z_motor_screw_0, y_rail_left_screw_0, y_rail_right_screw_0, z_rail_left_screw_2, z_rail_right_screw_2 ....
