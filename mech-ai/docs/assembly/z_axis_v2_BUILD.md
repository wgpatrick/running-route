# Build instructions: z_axis_v2

76 parts, 43 screws, about 13 minutes (rough allowance). Tools: 2.5 mm hex key, 4 mm hex key.

## Sub-assembly: drive

1. Start with **motor_mount** on the bench.
2. Fit **z_motor** onto motor_mount: bring it in from the side (-Y). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
3. Drive 4 screws from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: motor_screw_3, motor_screw_2, motor_screw_1, motor_screw_0.
4. Fit **coupler** onto z_motor: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).

## Main assembly

1. Start with **base_cross** on the bench.
2. Fit **upright_right** onto base_cross: bring it in from above (-Z).
3. Fit the **drive** sub-assembly onto upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
4. Drive 1 screw from the side with the 4 mm hex key: mount_bolt_right.
5. Fit **upright_left** onto base_cross, motor_mount: bring it in from above (-Z).
6. Drive 1 screw from the side with the 4 mm hex key: mount_bolt_left.
7. Fit **top_beam** onto upright_left, upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (nothing stops it falling).
8. Fit **bearing_plate** onto top_beam: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it would tip or slide off (centre of mass is 3 mm outside what it rests on)).
9. Drive 2 screws from above with the 4 mm hex key: bearing_plate_bolt_1, bearing_plate_bolt_0.
10. Fit **rail_right** onto motor_mount, upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it would tip or slide off (centre of mass is 0 mm outside what it rests on)).
11. Drive 2 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: rail_right_screw_2, rail_right_screw_0.
12. Fit **rail_left** onto motor_mount, upright_left: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it would tip or slide off (centre of mass is 0 mm outside what it rests on)).
13. Drive 2 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: rail_left_screw_2, rail_left_screw_0.
14. Fit **carriage_right_0** onto motor_mount, rail_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
15. Fit **carriage_left_0** onto motor_mount, rail_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
16. Fit **carrier** onto carriage_left_0, carriage_right_0: bring it in from the side (+Y). Hold it until fastened (it would tip or slide off (no surface underneath it)).
17. Fit **standoff_3** onto carrier: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it rests only on parts that are themselves still loose).
18. Fit **standoff_2** onto carrier: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it rests only on parts that are themselves still loose).
19. Fit **standoff_1** onto carrier: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it rests only on parts that are themselves still loose).
20. Fit **standoff_0** onto carrier: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it rests only on parts that are themselves still loose).
21. Fit **bed** onto standoff_0, standoff_1, standoff_2: bring it in from above (-Z). Hold it until fastened (it rests only on parts that are themselves still loose).
22. Drive 4 screws from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: bed_screw_3, bed_screw_2, bed_screw_1, bed_screw_0.
23. Fit **z_nut** onto carrier: bring it in from above (-Z).
24. Drive 3 screws from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: nut_screw_2, nut_screw_1, nut_screw_0.
25. Fit **carriage_right_1** onto carrier, rail_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
26. Drive 8 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: carriage_right_1_screw_3, carriage_right_1_screw_2, carriage_right_1_screw_1, carriage_right_1_screw_0, carriage_right_0_screw_3, carriage_right_0_screw_2, carriage_right_0_screw_1, carriage_right_0_screw_0.
27. Fit **carriage_left_1** onto carrier, rail_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
28. Drive 16 screws from the side with the 2.5 mm hex key: carriage_left_1_screw_3, carriage_left_1_screw_2, carriage_left_1_screw_1, carriage_left_1_screw_0, carriage_left_0_screw_3, carriage_left_0_screw_2, carriage_left_0_screw_1, carriage_left_0_screw_0 ....
29. Fit **base_right** onto base_cross, upright_right: bring it in from the side (-X).
30. Fit **base_left** onto base_cross, upright_left: bring it in from the side (+X).
31. Fit **leadscrew** onto coupler, z_nut: bring it in from above (-Z).
32. Fit **brkt_up_out_right** onto base_cross, base_right, upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
33. Fit **brkt_up_out_left** onto base_cross, base_left, upright_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
34. Fit **brkt_up_in_right** onto base_cross, upright_right: bring it in from the side (-Y).
35. Fit **brkt_up_in_left** onto base_cross, upright_left: bring it in from the side (-Y).
36. Fit **brkt_top_right** onto top_beam, upright_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
37. Fit **brkt_top_left** onto top_beam, upright_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
38. Fit **brkt_cross_back_right** onto base_cross, base_right: bring it in from above (-Z).
39. Fit **brkt_cross_back_left** onto base_cross, base_left: bring it in from above (-Z).
40. Fit **top_bearing** onto bearing_plate, leadscrew: bring it in from above (-Z).
