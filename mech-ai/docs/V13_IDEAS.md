# Ideas for the next version (after Build 1)

## Enclosure as an add-on (owner, 2026-11-20)

Not part of Build 1. Design it for the next version as a bolt-on to the 2020 frame.

- Panels: 3 mm polycarbonate (tough, doesn't crack when drilled; CNC-routed, since it laser-cuts poorly), held in the
  6 mm slots with stock panel clips; hinged front door; top panel.
- PLA/PETG only: the chamber must not run warm. Passive vents plus a small exhaust fan, Klipper-controlled from a chamber
  temperature sensor; the door can stay open for PLA. Model chamber temperature (the bed alone may warm a closed box to about
  35-40 degC) and hotend heat creep.
- Electronics bay (PSU, Pi, Octopus) stays outside the warm air or is separately vented.
- Benefits for the loop: stable ambient for the digital twin, stable lighting for the cameras (frosted or tinted panels plus
  the LED strip), a cover for the Class 1M laser, belts and hot parts out of reach, less dust and noise.
- Checks it must pass: clearance to the gantry and toolhead over full travel, camera sight lines, door access for
  maintenance, chamber and electronics temperatures, mass and cost (get real quotes; rough guess low hundreds of dollars).
- For Build 1: avoid permanently filling the frame slots and outer faces an enclosure would need, where it costs nothing.
