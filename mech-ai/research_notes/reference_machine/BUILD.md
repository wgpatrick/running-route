# Build instructions: ref_vminion

197 parts, 104 screws, about 41 minutes (rough allowance). Tools: 2 mm hex key, 2.5 mm hex key, 3 mm hex key, 4 mm hex key, 5 mm hex key, 6 mm hex key.

## Sub-assembly: x_drive

1. Start with **x_motor** on the bench, on its back (front face up).
2. Fit **x_motor_cage** onto x_motor: bring it in from above (+Y).
3. Drive 4 screws from above with the 2.5 mm hex key: x_motor_screw_3, x_motor_screw_2, x_motor_screw_1, x_motor_screw_0.
4. Fit **x_pulley** onto x_motor: bring it in from above (+Y).

## Sub-assembly: psu_panel
