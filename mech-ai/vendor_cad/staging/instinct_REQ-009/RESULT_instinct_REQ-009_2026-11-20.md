## Top 2 (REQ-009 — US sellers)

1. **HIWIN MGN12HZ0C set, 295 mm, $79.64 — Motion Constrained.** Genuine HIWIN (exact REQ-005 geometry: 8 mm rail / 13 mm assembly height, H long block), by far the cheapest genuine-HIWIN US route found (Digi-Key equivalent is $184 calc.). Official CAD + drawings already delivered in the REQ-005 results folder. Free US shipping. LENGTH NOTE: this seller's nearest length to the requested ~300 mm is 295 mm (5 mm short; they sell 25 mm steps and no 300 mm). The REQ says "about 300 mm", which 295 mm arguably satisfies, but it is NOT a true 300 mm rail. True-300 mm US alternatives: Misumi SSEBL13-300 set ($168.11, ships Nov 30) or Digi-Key MGNR12R-300 rail ($125.00) + block ($59.00) = $184.00 calc.
2. **Misumi SSELBZ13-295, $130.31 (ships Nov 30) — Misumi USA.** US-stocked ball-guide set with CAD on the page and Misumi's configurator. Caveat: rail height 7.5 mm (0.5 mm lower than HIWIN), slight-clearance (no preload) only. If exact 300 mm matters, SSEBL13-300 at $168.11 is the true-300 mm Misumi option.

## Top 3 overall (REQ-009b — cost, play/precision, US stock, CAD)
