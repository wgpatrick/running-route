These are report input assumptions, not manufacturer guarantees. The manufacturer
brochure table above does not specify density or specific heat. Howard Precision's
product page says 2.7 g/cm^3 and 0.101 lb/in^3, which are not mutually consistent;
no single density is certified here. Request a lot/material certificate for mass-
critical work instead of silently mixing these sources.

Do not substitute 6061/ATP5 properties for MIC6. The earlier UPMet PDF had blank
numeric tables on visual inspection; this readable source and text replace it for
property lookup. Brochure reuse licence not stated; manufacturer copyright applies.
