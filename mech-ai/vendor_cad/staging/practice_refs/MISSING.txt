UNRESOLVED FIELDS / LIMITS OF THE COLLECTION
Collected 15 November 2026. These are gaps in the sources checked, not claims that no such data exist anywhere.

3. A definitive current ISO 4762 standard length-range matrix is not verified. Aramfix's detailed matrix is labeled DIN 912 despite its ISO 4762 cover; Lederer is a catalogue availability matrix.
4. No validated permissible surface-pressure table for printed PETG/ABS under screw heads. Bossard's VDI tables cover metals and distinguish 1986 from 2015 values.
5. No validation that Bossard's M6-M16 engagement trials apply to M2.5/M3. Material, screw class, tolerance and effective engagement matter.
6. The requested washer size range does not exist in every named series: ISO 7090 starts at M5; ISO 7093-1 scope starts at M3. M2.5 in a maker's large-washer catalogue must not be relabeled as a standard ISO 7093-1 size.
7. SPIROL pull-out/torque-out test values are for its named inserts and nylon test conditions, not Ruthex inserts or arbitrary printed PETG/ABS. A matched part/process test is still needed for a design allowable.
8. SKF axial guidance is conditional and has a small-bearing exception. Fits depend on load and ring motion. No single universal 608 service axial-load/fit combination is established.
9. Separate, explicitly identified MG block-to-carriage bolt torque not verified. Rail torque is not block torque.
10. No controlled set-screw holding comparison with versus without a shaft flat verified. Flat-point screw terminology does not fill this gap.
11. Ruland 8 mm collar dimensions included, but no matching verified holding-force rating. DIN 471 supplied as the circlip alternative, not a separate DIN 6799 table.
12. Exact legacy Gates GT2 2 mm pulley/idler/mesh/tension-by-width coverage remains incomplete. Current Gates tables use GT3 belts with GT2 sprockets. They must not be relabeled as GT2 or generic printer-belt values.
13. No generic T8x8 four-start screw/nut accuracy or backlash guarantee. Maker references apply to their listed thread, nut, material and support arrangement.
14. No universal long-term allowable stress for printed PETG/ABS. ABS creep research is process-specific; PETG research uses pellet extrusion, not Prusament filament. HDT and unloaded aging are not creep allowables.
16. Maker ISO 13715 summary has a B.7 source inconsistency: diagram/notation show 0.1-0.5 mm, text says 0.1-0.3 mm. Flagged in the saved snapshot; the licensed standard is needed to settle released drawing requirements.
17. Electrical summaries are not the complete applicable standard, local code or compliance verification. General IP2X enclosure guidance is kept separate from residual-voltage exceptions at exposed plug conductors.
